Frequently Asked Questions

What is Deal Theka?

Deal Theka is a local digital marketplace platform connecting customers with local workers and service providers.

Who can register on Deal Theka?

Electricians, plumbers, drivers, mechanics, carpenters, technicians, and local businesses can register.

Is Deal Theka free to use?

Yes, customers can search and discover services freely.

How does booking work?

Users can search services, view details, and directly contact or book service providers.

Does Deal Theka support mobile devices?

Yes, the platform is mobile-first and optimized for Android devices.

How secure is the platform?

We use authentication systems, secure cloud infrastructure, and backend technologies for data protection.

Will AI features be added in future?

Yes, future versions may include AI-powered recommendations, multilingual support, smart filtering, and automation.
