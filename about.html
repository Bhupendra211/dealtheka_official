About Deal Theka

Deal Theka is a next-generation local service marketplace platform designed to connect customers with trusted local workers and service providers using mobile technology and digital infrastructure.

Our Vision

We believe millions of skilled workers in India deserve better digital access, visibility, and employment opportunities. Deal Theka aims to create a transparent, efficient, and accessible ecosystem for local services.

Who Can Use Deal Theka?

  • Electricians
  • Plumbers
  • Drivers
  • Carpenters
  • Mechanics
  • Daily Wage Workers
  • Local Businesses

Technology Behind Deal Theka

The platform uses modern technologies such as Flutter, Firebase, cloud backend services, scalable architecture, and future AI-ready integrations.

Our goal is to simplify local service discovery while improving employment opportunities and digital inclusion across India.
